Professional History

From Passion to Strategy

My journey started in gastronomy, where I learned to appreciate how passion can turn ideas into meaningful experiences. Along the way, I discovered another passion: understanding how businesses work and what makes them succeed.

Through my experience solving business problems, I began noticing a pattern. Many companies struggle not because their products lack value, but because their internal processes, marketing strategies, and customer experiences aren’t aligned.

That realization sparked my passion for go-to-market strategy, modern marketing, and business growth.

I’m fascinated by understanding the customer behind my client’s customer, identifying opportunities, and building systems that connect strategy, technology, and execution.

Because sustainable growth doesn’t happen by accident. It happens when the right strategy, the right systems, and a deep understanding of the customer work together.

My goalTo help businesses turn complexity into clarity, challenges into opportunities, and strategy into sustainable growth.

The chapters

How I got here

Every job I've had taught me something about people. Swipe, scroll sideways or use the arrows.

  1. 2021

    Instituto Gato Dumas2021–2023

    It started in a kitchen

    Before I ever opened a CRM, I was training in culinary arts at Instituto Gato Dumas. A kitchen teaches you fast: every plate goes to a real person, and you know right away whether you got it right. I didn't know it then, but that was my first lesson in customer success.

    What I took with me: people come back when they feel looked after.

  2. 2023

    Major League InsuranceMay 2023 – Jan 2024

    The calls nobody wants to take

    My first job in business was on the retention line at Major League Insurance. Almost every call started the same way: someone wanted to cancel. I learned to stop defending and start listening. Most of the time the problem wasn't the customer. Something had broken long before they picked up the phone.

    What I took with me: every complaint has a story behind it, and that's where the fix is.

  3. 2024

    Vital Insurance CompanyJan 2024 – Dec 2024

    One month later, I was leading the floor

    I joined Vital Insurance Company as a representative. A month later I was the floor supervisor. Suddenly I wasn't taking the calls anymore; I was helping a team of 10 to 15 people take theirs. I stepped in on the hardest calls and coached people through the rest.

    What I took with me: a team performs best when someone is right there with them, not just watching the numbers.

  4. 2025

    FedExJan 2025 – Jan 2026

    Learning how buyers decide

    At FedEx I spent my days talking with people deciding whether to buy. Some said yes in minutes. Others needed a reason to trust us. I started to see patterns: what made people hesitate, what gave them confidence, what happened when nobody followed up.

    What I took with me: I was already studying the client of my client.

  5. 2026

    Scale Up Labs · RIVAS LLC · VGO2026 – Present

    Where it all comes together

    Today I run client operations at Scale Up Labs, with up to 15 accounts at a time. Every chapter shows up in my day: I listen like I did on the retention line, lead like I did on the floor and read buyers like I did at FedEx. I also design the systems that let a whole team work that way. At RIVAS LLC, I bring the same approach to AI and marketing projects.

    What I took with me: the kitchen was right. People come back when they feel looked after.
